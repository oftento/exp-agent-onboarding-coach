# Global / Country-Specific eXp Onboarding Reference

Use this reference when an agent asks whether eXp onboarding differs by country/region. Yes — onboarding differs materially because licensing, broker/entity structure, worker classification, local real estate law, MLS/board equivalents, taxes, fees, language, support channels, and tool availability vary by jurisdiction.

## Global rule

Never assume the U.S. onboarding process applies globally. Start by identifying the country/region, then route the agent to the country-specific eXp site, join form, eXp International webinar/training, local broker/country team, and local regulator/association requirements.

## Universal onboarding pattern

Most eXp markets still follow this general pattern:

1. Country/region selection and join/application form.
2. Local license/registration/eligibility check.
3. Country broker/entity/team review.
4. Local independent-contractor, representative, agency, or employment-style paperwork depending on law.
5. Payment/tax/banking setup where applicable.
6. Local board/MLS/portal/association or regulatory setup where applicable.
7. eXp systems access: Passport/Okta or local equivalent, My eXp/Enterprise legacy, Workplace, eXp World/eXpert Care, eXp University/international hub, local agent center if available.
8. Local training: country onboarding, compliance, contracts/forms, transaction workflow, marketing rules, anti-money-laundering requirements where applicable.
9. Business launch: CRM/website/lead generation/marketing resources, subject to local availability.

## What changes by country

| Area | Why it changes |
|---|---|
| License/registration | Some markets require state/province licenses; others use national registration, broker supervision, estate-agent authority, or different commercial-agent models. |
| Broker/entity | Local eXp entity, principal broker, licensed agent, country director, state/province broker team, or equivalent support structure differs. |
| Agent classification | eXp corporate filings say most agents are independent contractors except where local law requires otherwise. |
| Fees/taxes | VAT/GST/IVA, local business registration, tax forms, and fee schedules vary and may not be public. |
| MLS/portal/board setup | U.S./Canada MLS/board models differ from portals/associations/regulators in other markets. |
| Compliance | Advertising, AML, data privacy, client money, trust accounts, agency agreements, and disclosure rules vary. |
| Tools | CRM of Choice (BoldTrail/Cloze/Lofty), transaction tools, portals, lead products, luxury/referral programs, and local agent centers may not be uniform. Verify country availability and included vs paid add-ons. |
| Training/language | Country training may be English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Arabic, or other local language. |

## Important caveats

- Public country pages do not consistently publish full fee schedules, caps, split details, tool availability, or onboarding steps.
- Some country sites are coming soon, login-gated, Cloudflare-protected, or app-rendered.
- Use official country webinars/join forms and country teams for current instructions.
- Keep local legal/regulatory questions outside the AI answer and route them to the local broker/team or regulator.
