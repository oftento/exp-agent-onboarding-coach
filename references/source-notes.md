## Practical research conclusions

1. eXp onboarding is sequential but local: join application → license/registration verification → local broker/country-team approval → local agreements/document revisions → affiliation/transfer/registration → active in eXp systems → welcome email/tools.
2. New agents often feel tool overload: Passport/Okta, My eXp, Enterprise legacy workflows, Workplace, eXp World, eXp email, eXp University, CRM of Choice (BoldTrail/Cloze/Lofty), SkySlope, Marketing Center, VersaPay, state/MLS systems.
3. CRM of Choice should be checked before agents pay for outside CRM. BoldTrail, Cloze, and Lofty may be included in the existing monthly tech package for standard use, but add-ons, premium services, IDX/MLS feed fees, upgraded plans, switching rules, and country availability can vary.
4. SkySlope requires training and mock practice before live files.
5. Mentor support can be valuable but should be clarified: expectations, cadence, split/cost, graduation requirements, and fit.
6. Sponsor/upline choice is consequential and should be verified before activation using official eXp support/onboarding, not unofficial summaries.
7. Best coaching approach: stage the onboarding path into today/this week/first 30 days, with a support directory and clear broker-escalation habits.
8. Best tool-discovery approach: before recommending outside software, route the agent through My eXp, Passport/Okta, eXp Knowledge Base, Workplace, eXp World/eXpert Care, eXp University, eXp Events Calendar, and relevant eXp programs.

## Compliance sources and principles

Use current official sources when giving compliance-sensitive guidance:

- Federal Fair Housing Act / HUD / DOJ fair housing resources.
- NAR Code of Ethics if the agent is a REALTOR.
- State real estate commission rules.
- Local association and MLS rules.
- Brokerage policies and local broker/country team instructions.
- FTC advertising guidance and relevant phone/text/email consent rules.

The skill intentionally uses conservative compliance guardrails and escalates state/broker/MLS-specific issues.

## CRM of Choice update — July 2026

A user correction highlighted that many eXp agents do not know they may be able to use **BoldTrail, Cloze, and Lofty** through eXp's **CRM of Choice** program without an additional standard-use CRM fee. The skill now treats CRM discovery as a day-one tool-discovery item rather than defaulting to kvCORE/BoldTrail or a paid outside CRM.

### Sources and caveats

- eXp Toolkit CRM of Choice: https://exptoolkit.com/crmofchoice — lists BoldTrail, Lofty, and Cloze as CRM choices included in the existing monthly tech package with no additional fees for standard use; says access is through Okta SSO and includes CRM-specific onboarding/training.
- eXp University CRM of Choice: https://www.expuniversity.com/crm-of-choice — provides training for BoldTrail, Lofty, and Cloze.
- eXp Cloud Support “What is BoldTrail?”: https://support.expcloud.com/portal/en/kb/articles/what-is-boldtrail — says active eXp Realty agents can opt into BoldTrail at no cost; notes possible MLS IDX feed fees and paid BoldTrail Marketplace services.
- Cloze announcement: https://ai.cloze.com/resource-center/exp-realty-selects-cloze/ — states eXp agents in the U.S., Canada, and Puerto Rico can select Cloze at no additional cost as part of the monthly tech package.
- Lofty announcement: https://lofty.com/news/exp-crm-of-choice — states Lofty is part of eXp's CRM of Choice and included within the existing monthly tech package.

Phrase this carefully: “included/no additional fee for standard use through the existing eXp monthly tech package” rather than “completely free for every agent everywhere.” Add-ons, premium services, IDX/MLS feed fees, upgraded plans, country availability, and switching/renewal rules may vary.
