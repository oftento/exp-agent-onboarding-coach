# QA Checklist — eXp Agent Onboarding Coach Output

Use this checklist before delivering any plan, script, checklist, or marketing review generated with the skill.

## Scope

- [ ] The agent's country/region and status are identified or reasonable assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Country/state/province/MLS/portal/broker-specific items are marked for verification.
- [ ] The output does not claim to be official eXp policy unless sourced.
- [ ] The output gives next actions, not just explanation.

## eXp onboarding and tool/resource coverage

- [ ] Join/application or activation status addressed when relevant.
- [ ] Global/country-specific onboarding differences are handled when outside the U.S. or unknown.
- [ ] Country-specific guidance avoids applying U.S.-only steps unless verified.
- [ ] License transfer/activation risk addressed when relevant.
- [ ] Passport/Okta, My eXp, Enterprise legacy status, Workplace, eXp World/eXpert Care mentioned when relevant.
- [ ] Tool/resource questions route through internal eXp discovery before recommending outside software.
- [ ] Resource recommendations include access path and caveats: login-gated, paid, eligibility-based, region/MLS-specific, or renamed.
- [ ] Training path includes New Agent Orientation / eXp University when relevant.
- [ ] CRM guidance checks CRM of Choice first: BoldTrail, Cloze, and Lofty.
- [ ] No-additional-fee/free language is caveated: standard use may be included in existing tech package; add-ons, IDX/MLS fees, upgrades, switching rules, and country availability may vary.
- [ ] SkySlope/transaction readiness included for first-deal situations.
- [ ] Mentor/local broker/country team support path included.

## Compliance

- [ ] No legal, tax, lending, appraisal, inspection, or broker-policy advice presented as final authority.
- [ ] No fair housing steering, protected-class targeting, or coded advertising language.
- [ ] No income, sales, lead, or timeline guarantees.
- [ ] No instruction to practice before license is active/affiliated.
- [ ] No instruction to automate outreach without consent/DNC/TCPA/CAN-SPAM review.
- [ ] Broker/legal/MLS escalation included where needed.

## Practicality

- [ ] Output includes checklists or scripts where useful.
- [ ] Output separates today, this week, and later actions.
- [ ] Output is not overwhelming.
- [ ] Output ends with the next 1–5 concrete actions.
